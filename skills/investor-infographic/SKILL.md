## Workflow

1. Research current data before writing images. For holdings, prefer SEC 13F, fund websites, WhaleWisdom, Nasdaq/official filings, and clearly record the quarter/date. For companies, prefer annual reports, earnings releases, investor relations, and market data with exact dates.
   For company branding, the logo/wordmark must be downloaded from the company's official website, official brand page, investor-relations page, or official annual report. `必须从公司官网`取得原始品牌资产并登记`官方资产来源 URL`、下载日期和文件哈希。`禁止手工重绘` Logo、凭印象描摹、用股票代码/中文简称代替、使用第三方 Logo 库截图，或把“看起来相似”当作已核验。没有找到官方资产时，停止品牌图渲染并继续查证，不得自行造一个临时徽章。
2. Convert all monetary figures to U.S. dollars before writing image text. Clearly label dollar values as `美元`, `USD`, or `$...`; do not leave revenue, profit, market cap, holdings value, cash, CapEx, dividends, buybacks, or debt in local currency as the main displayed value.
3. Separate stable operating data from dynamic market data. Full-year revenue, profit, cash flow, segment revenue/profit, and ROI/ROIC should use the latest complete fiscal year. For the key operating cards, add small year-over-year percentages when reliable prior-year data is available, especially for revenue, operating profit, and operating cash flow. If the fiscal year differs from the calendar year, keep the main visual label short, such as `FY2024 营收`, `FY2025 营收`, or `FY2026 营收`, and put the fiscal-year period only in the bottom footer. Stock price, market cap, PE/PB/PS, dividend yield, ranking, and analyst target prices must be re-checked at generation time; show only short labels such as `当前市值` or `PE` in the main visual, with the as-of period only in the bottom footer.
4. For company operating-analysis images, include a full-year profitability-return metric by default. Prefer company-level ROIC: `NOPAT / average invested capital`, where invested capital is `total debt + total equity - cash and equivalents`, averaged across beginning/end fiscal-year balance sheets. If NOPAT is not available, use `net income / average invested capital` and label it `ROI（净利润口径）`. Do not confuse this with stock-price total return. If reliable invested-capital inputs are unavailable, show `ROI口径：资料不足/不可比` rather than inventing a number, or use the documented financial-industry exception below.
   For financial companies, a sourced ROE/ROA/ROTCE may replace the lower ROI card when invested-capital returns are not comparable; record the industry exception and keep the actual metric name. Put the formula, fiscal-year period, source, and any limitation in the bottom footer, not inside metric cards.
   Also include a `2026 CapEx/资本开支` item by default in company operating-analysis or operating-panorama images. Use company guidance, annual-report capex plans, earnings-call commentary, investor-day materials, or credible analyst/market-data estimates with an exact date. If a company does not disclose a reliable 2026 CapEx figure, write `2026 CapEx：未披露/不可比` or a clearly labeled directional note instead of inventing a number. Put the CapEx source/date and limitation in the bottom footer.
   Include a `股东分红/股东回报` item when building company operating data. Keep the current annual dividend/current or forward yield display and a smaller `近10年平均股息率` line. Use a reproducible same-security series or annual cash dividends divided by each year-end price; preserve observations, split basis and averaging formula. Do not mix daily and year-end samples. With fewer comparable years use `可比期均值` and the actual period; with no reliable history report `资料不足`, never invent values. Non-dividend years count as `0%`; distinguish a verified zero from unavailable data. Record method, sources and dates in the footer/packet. See the approved reference for calculation boundaries.
5. For every company set, enforce `公司档案九项必填` in this fixed order: `公司全称、成立时间、创始人 / 创立主体、总部地址、交易所及代码、CEO / 主要负责人、员工人数、核心业务、当前市值`. The founder field must use an official company history page, filing, or equivalent primary source. For mergers, joint ventures, state-established entities, and spin-offs, write the truthful founding entity or formation mechanism instead of inventing an individual founder. Never omit a required field to improve layout; use `未披露` only when a reliable public value truly cannot be found. PE and Forward PE belong in `经营全景`, not in the company-profile block. A predecessor, rebrand, listing history, or other useful context may appear only after the nine required fields.
   The listed-company `公司档案` must also include a hand-drawn `上市以来股价走势` section immediately after `业务范围` and before `行业地位与核心亮点`. Use a continuous public market-price series from the actual listing, relisting, merger, or currently comparable security; state both the corporate listing event and the chart-data start when they differ, rather than implying unavailable history. Plot split-adjusted closing prices, explicitly state that cash-dividend reinvestment is excluded, and record the provider, ticker/security, price period, and retrieval date in the footer. Display the chart in USD. For non-USD listings, record a dated conversion method; if one current fixed FX rate is applied to the entire historical series only to unify the visual scale, say that explicitly and do not describe it as historical-date FX conversion.
6. For company sets, add the fourth `管理文化` image using `references/management-culture-evidence.md`. Prioritize distinctive employee treatment and concrete, learnable organization/ethics/customer commitments, not general business strategy. Prefer 1-2 strong practices, maximum 3; never force a count. Each card needs a specific `做法：`, supporting `案例：` or `数字：`, original sources, and research-only `distinctiveness`, `evidence_scope`, `evidence_status` fields. A published rule is not proof of execution; a hiring or R&D headcount is not proof of good employee treatment. Superiority claims require comparable benchmarks. If nothing qualifies, report the evidence gap instead of inventing a card. Preserve existing company colors, semantic hand-drawn icons and layout style; do not add career-advice, employee/investor viewpoints, investment implications or a repeated bottom summary.
7. Create or update a data module in `/Users/jiangming/templates`, named `data_<slug>.py`. Match the relevant schema exactly: investor keys for `investor_infographic.py`, company keys for `company_infographic.py`.
8. Add detailed source metadata to the data module, not just generic labels like "年报" or "彭博". Keep footer dates visually compact: prefer `2025年1-12月`, `2026年1-3月`, or `2026年5月` instead of full repeated day ranges like `2025-01-01 至 2025-12-31`; use day-level dates only when precision is essential and the line still reads cleanly. Use `FOOTER_LINES` whenever possible with separate centered lines for:
   - `经营数据：<company annual report / 10-K / 20-F / annual results / earnings release>，FY2025: <YYYY年M-M月>`
   - `行情数据：<market-data provider such as StockAnalysis / CompaniesMarketCap / Yahoo Finance / Nasdaq / Wind>，查看/截至：<YYYY年M月>`

   - `2026 CapEx口径：<company guidance / annual report / earnings call / investor day / credible estimate>，截至/查看：<YYYY年M月>`; if not disclosed, state `未披露/不可比`

   - `免责声明：本图仅供学习参考，不构成投资建议`
   - `by 江明`

## Style Rules

- Design priority for company images: keep the main visual clean and designer-like. Metric cards and section labels should use short display text such as `FY20XX 营收`, `FY20XX 净利润`, `当前市值`, `2026E CapEx`, and `ROIC`, where `FY20XX` follows the latest complete fiscal year in the source. Do not put long fiscal-year ranges, source names, exchange-rate details, ROI formulas, or CapEx source notes in the visible cards or hero area; put those audit details in the bottom footer.
- In company operating cards, small YoY labels can sit inside the card below the main label, such as `YoY +12.4%` or `同比 -6.1%`. Keep the percentage smaller than the main number and only add it to the most meaningful operating metrics, usually revenue, operating profit, and operating cash flow.
- In `经营全景`, keep the upper metric grid fixed to six default metric categories: latest complete fiscal-year revenue, operating profit, operating cash flow, net profit, current market cap, and `PE / Forward PE`. The first four labels should use the real fiscal-year prefix from the source, such as `FY2024 营收`, `FY2025 营收`, or `FY2026 营收`; do not hard-code `FY2025` when the latest complete fiscal year differs. Add small YoY text inside the first four cards when reliable. Do not add separate upper cards for PS, PB, margin, cash, debt, product volume, users, ROI/ROIC, 2026 CapEx, annual dividend, buyback, payout ratio, or shareholder return. Margins may appear as small secondary text inside profit cards, and PS/PB may appear only in the footer or as an exception when PE is unavailable.
- Treat `经营全景` as a sectioned dashboard: top grid = the six-card financial + valuation snapshot; middle = segment/product mix; lower = ROI/ROIC + 2026 CapEx + shareholder distribution/capital allocation; bottom = highlights/risks. Each metric should normally appear once in its most relevant section.
- The `经营全景` middle `业务分部表现` section must be real business, product, geography, or customer-segment mix with revenue/value evidence and visible percentages. Do not use quarterly KPI rows, generic financial rows such as `1Q26 营收` / `FY2025 净利润`, valuation metrics, customer contra-revenue rows, ROI/ROIC, CapEx, dividends, buybacks, or shareholder-return rows in this section. If the company discloses no clean segment mix, use product mix, revenue-mix estimates clearly labeled in the footer, or reduce the row count rather than filling with unrelated metrics.
- `经营全景` 中部必须先定义“这些行是什么”，再显示占比。每个百分比必须在可见区域写清分母，例如`占FY2025集团总营收`；禁止只写`14%`这类无分母数字。
- 只有法定/管理报告分部才能命名为`业务分部`或`报告分部`。Top产品、Top品牌、治疗领域、地区或客户结构必须用准确标题，例如`核心产品净销售构成`、`按治疗领域总收入构成`，不得统称`业务分部表现`。
- 完整收入构成必须闭环到100%（四舍五入容差99%-101%）。只展示Top项目时，必须同时显示Top项目合计覆盖率和`其他产品/品牌/地区`行；抵销前分部必须明确标注`抵销前`，并单列`未分配`、`其他`、`合并抵销`等调节项，使金额与集团总额闭环。
- 英文药品名、业务名或分部缩写必须紧跟简短中文括注，说明药品主要治疗什么或业务主要做什么，例如`Entresto（心衰药）`、`Cloud Intelligence（云与AI服务）`。不得让普通读者只面对英文专有名词。
- 对新建或刷新的公司页，数据包必须提供`panorama.segment_mix`：`section_title`、`denominator`、`basis`、`mode`、`reconciliation`。在渲染前运行可执行validator；分母、名称、覆盖率或抵销闭环任一失败时禁止出图。
- For financial companies or special industries where operating profit or operating cash flow is not meaningful, replace the affected top-grid cards with industry equivalents such as net revenue, pre-provision profit, ROTCE, CET1, or another clearly labeled standard metric; keep the top grid at six cards unless the user explicitly asks otherwise.
- Orange long image: warm orange system, `#E8883A` as the main accent, generated by Pillow at `SCALE=3` and 2400px width.
- Hand-drawn images: capture the HTML's actual CSS canvas. Landscape sketches may be 1200x750 at 4x; company portrait sketches are often 900x2020 or 900x1980 and should be captured at 3x or higher so the full page and footer are visible.
- Hand-drawn background: light paper `#F7F4ED` with subtle grid lines, inline SVG doodles, rounded hand-drawn strokes, and no stock-looking art.
- Keep information density high but readable: key numbers, top holdings/business segments, distribution chart, changes/actions, philosophy/moat, quotes, and a clear bottom insight.
- Keep colors disciplined: no more than three dominant colors per image unless the existing reference uses a broader palette intentionally.
- When updating existing company hand-drawn templates, treat the old company-specific visual style as the base contract. Preserve the company's palette, logo-like mark, doodle icons, hand-drawn motifs, title hierarchy, and overall layout unless the user explicitly asks for a redesign. New requirements such as ROI/ROIC, 2026 CapEx, source-footers, or management-culture notes should be added as patches to the existing style, not by replacing every company with a unified generic template.
- `核心业务与硬指标`采用`最小补丁原则`：对已有公司图片，保留原卡片标题、配色、图标、位置、标题层级与解释语气；通常只在原卡片中新增一行短的`量化证据`。不得为了补数据而重写整张图、改成统一文案、把原有业务标题换成泛化的`规模收入底盘`、`利润与现金流`、`核心护城河`或`客户与渠道`。
- In company hand-drawn sets, keep `经营之道` and `经营全景` distinct. `经营之道` should explain why the business can compound: moat, flywheel, customer lock-in, technology route, strategic moves, and business logic. `经营全景` should be the data dashboard: revenue/profit, segment mix, ROI/ROIC, 2026 CapEx, risks, and capital allocation. Avoid copying the same metric cards and paragraphs across both images.
- `经营之道` must be understandable to a normal reader: first state the company's long-term strategic positioning, then show the few truly core money-making drivers. The opening thesis should answer what the company does, what core capability it relies on, which customer/scenario it enters, what value it creates, and how it can monetize over time.
- In `经营之道`, use 1-5 evidence-backed core items, usually 3-4; fewer strong items are better than padding. Preserve existing section titles; use a count such as `四个复利引擎` only when it matches the real card count.
- The main purpose of `经营之道` core-item cards is to prove moat strength, not merely describe product lines. Before writing these cards, actively look for each core business's strongest public moat evidence: official or credible market share, global/category rank, near-monopoly share, installed base, certification cycle, customer concentration, capacity, pricing power, retention, patents/R&D scale, or strategic customer commitments. If a product has an absolute moat indicator such as `90%份额`, `接近100%`, `全球第一`, `全球第二`, or similar category dominance, that figure must appear in the visible core card metric line, not only in the footer or internal notes.
- A core-card metric must be directly tied to the named business or capability. Current market cap, CompaniesMarketCap rank, employee count, generic company-wide revenue/profit, or a bare phrase such as `全球龙头` are not moat evidence. Company-wide financial data is allowed only when the card makes the business link explicit, for example `HBM收入占DS 42%` or `Keytruda $29B，占制药收入约X%`. If no public quantitative proof is available, reduce the number of core cards or call the item a normal business; never label it `护城河` merely to fill a layout.
- Do not substitute internal revenue mix for market-share evidence when market-share or category-rank data is publicly available. Revenue mix can be secondary context, but the headline metric should show why the item is a moat: share, rank, lock-in, installed base, certification barrier, or another competitive-strength indicator.
- Core items may be products, technologies, supply-chain advantages, brand/channel power, network effects, management culture, capital allocation, cost curves, customer lock-in, or ecosystem advantages, but only if they are central to long-term earnings. Supporting conditions such as generic partnerships, CapEx, financing capacity, or compute supply belong in `经营全景` or risk/capital sections unless they are themselves the company's core product or moat.
- Each `经营之道` core-item card should include a matching hand-drawn icon, a hard metric or public evidence, and one concise explanation of why it matters. Match icons semantically: chip/GPU for compute chips, stacked memory for HBM/storage, chain/factory/logistics for supply chain, badge/storefront for brand/channel, node network for ecosystem, portfolio/cash-flow for capital allocation, shield/pool for insurance float, contract/handshake for enterprise lock-in, and code/terminal for developer platforms.
- Store each visible core-card hard metric in an explicit structured field when the generator supports one; include its `evidence_kind` and source. Do not rely on regex extraction from prose as the source of truth. The review must reject a core card whose metric line merely repeats or truncates its description instead of showing a number, rank, share, retention rate, installed base, or another clearly labeled public evidence item. If an explicit metric mapping is present but a displayed core item has no mapping, fail rendering instead of silently falling back to prose or substituting company rank/employee count.
- The lower part of `经营之道` should prefer recent 2-3 year strategic actions when they exist: acquisitions, capacity expansion, R&D programs, product launches, customer or supplier commitments, market entry, capital allocation, or explicit investor-day targets.
